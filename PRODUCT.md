## Product Principles

1. Evidence over adjectives: every claim points to a role, a number, a certification, or a piece of writing.
2. Depth, honestly bounded: show the specialization's depth and say plainly what is still developing.
3. Legible to L1 and CISO alike: technical specifics available, business context first.
4. Quiet confidence: calm, considered, no hacker theatrics.
5. Respect confidentiality: client work described by outcome, never by sensitive detail.
